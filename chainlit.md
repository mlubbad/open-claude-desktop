# Open Claude Desktop 🤖
## Welcome to your GLM-powered BigQuery data agent!

What I can do:

- **🧠 Reasoning (ReAct):**  I think step-by-step to solve complex data problems.
- **🛠️ Human-in-the-Loop:** I will ask for your approval before running any SQL queries.
- **📎 Multimodal:** You can upload CSVs, PDFs, and Images for me to analyze alongside BigQuery data.

## How to use me:

Type a high-level goal (e.g., "Compare push notification dismissals in Egypt and Sudan yesterday").
Review the plan I propose and click "Approve".
Approve the SQL queries I generate.
Review the final analytical report!
Tip: Use the sidebar on the left to view your past chat history.
